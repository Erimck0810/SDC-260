Nomad Life Gallery

A functional e-commerce website concept for Nomad Life Gallery, the retail storefront division of Nomad Life LLC. 
The site allows visitors to browse original artwork and prints from independent artists, view detailed product information, 
and complete a purchase through a simple checkout flow.

Purpose

Nomad Life Gallery exists to give independent artists a storefront where their work 
can be sold directly to buyers, with full product details and a straightforward purchasing experience. 
This project is a basic, functional build of that concept — a four-page site demonstrating the core structure 
and navigation flow a real store would need: discovery, product detail, and checkout.

Feature Roadmap

Planned in four weekly milestones:

Week 1 — Store Homepage
 Homepage layout: nav bar, hero section, featured-works preview
 Navigation links to Browse and other pages
 Featured-works preview pulling from static product data
 Responsive layout check
 
Week 2 — Browse Page & Product Detail Page
 Static product data file (title, price, description, image, etc.)
 Browse page rendering the full product grid from that data
 Product Detail page template with dynamic content per item
 Linked navigation from Browse cards to their Product Detail pages
 
Week 3 — Checkout Page
 Checkout form (shipping and payment fields)
 Order summary showing the selected product and price
 Client-side form validation
 "Place order" action leading to a confirmation state
 
Week 4 — Confirmation Page, Polish & Submission Prep
 Order confirmation page with purchase recap
 Cross-page styling consistency pass
 Navigation and link fixes across all pages
 Final review and packaging for submission
